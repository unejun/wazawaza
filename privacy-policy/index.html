2. Personal Information We Collect

The Company may collect the following information directly from you, automatically through the Website and forms, from transaction service providers, or from public sources and commercial databases:

  • Applicant and offer information: name, country of residence, intended delivery country and locality, organization name and representative information (for entity applicants), the applicable Work, Offer Amount, preferred edition number, and messages submitted with an Initial Offer.
  • Contact and inquiry information: email address, telephone number, social-media handle, inquiry details, and records of emails, messages, support and other communications.
  • Selection and transaction information: Selection Notice, Checkout Information, Final Purchase Offer, Order Confirmation, full legal name, delivery address, recipient details, and information needed for manufacture, insurance, export, customs and delivery.
  • Payment and escrow information: payment status, wire-transfer references, transaction identifiers, escrow status, inspection or dispute information, and related records. The Company does not collect online-banking passwords or card security credentials.
  • Verification and compliance information: identity and address verification status, organization and beneficial-owner information, source-of-funds information where required, and results of anti-social-forces, sanctions, fraud-prevention and other compliance checks. As a general rule, original identity-verification documents are collected and retained directly by the designated Escrow Provider, and the Company receives only the status and limited information reasonably necessary for the transaction.
  • Technical information: IP address, browser and device information, access logs, cookies and similar identifiers, as described in Section 10.
  • Marketing records: the email address used for marketing, the date and content of the consent, and subscription or withdrawal records.

If information required for an Initial Offer, selection, verification, payment, shipment or other transaction step is not provided, the Company may be unable to receive or evaluate the offer, select the Applicant, form or perform a Sale Contract, or deliver the Work.

3. Purposes of Use

The Company uses Personal Information only to the extent necessary for the following purposes:

  1. To receive, manage and evaluate Initial Offers, conduct the curated selection process, and communicate selection, non-selection, expiry or related decisions.
  2. To assess availability, destination and delivery feasibility, calculate shipping and related charges, provide the Total Purchase Amount and Checkout Information, receive a Final Purchase Offer, issue an Order Confirmation, and form and perform a Sale Contract.
  3. To conduct identity, anti-social-forces, sanctions, fraud-prevention, anti-money-laundering, source-of-funds, export-control, import and other compliance checks.
  4. To administer payment and escrow, manufacture and pack the Work, arrange insurance, export, customs and delivery, and process inspection-period objections, returns or insurance claims where applicable.
  5. To respond to inquiries, provide transaction and after-sales support, prevent or investigate misuse, and establish, exercise or defend legal rights.
  6. To operate, maintain and secure the Website and forms, analyze their use, diagnose errors, and improve the Project and related services.
  7. To maintain accounting, tax, customs, export, transaction and other records required by law or reasonably necessary for business administration and dispute prevention.
  8. To provide information about the Project, related works and future editions only where separate, optional marketing consent has been given.

The Company will not use Personal Information beyond the scope reasonably related to these purposes without obtaining consent, except as permitted by the APPI or other applicable law.

4. Optional Marketing

Consent to marketing is optional, is requested separately from the confirmations required for an Initial Offer or purchase, and is not selected by default. Refusing or withdrawing marketing consent does not affect the receipt or evaluation of an Initial Offer. Consent may be withdrawn at any time through the unsubscribe method stated in the communication or by contacting the Privacy Contact.

5. Outsourcing and Provision to Third Parties

Outsourcing. Where a service provider handles Personal Data solely on the Company's behalf and within the purposes stated above, the Company may entrust such handling to contractors such as form and website providers, cloud and email providers, production workshops, and carriers. The Company selects appropriate contractors, includes necessary data-protection obligations in its arrangements, and exercises necessary and appropriate supervision.

Provision to Independent Third Parties. Where an escrow, payment, identity-verification, insurance, customs, logistics, professional, or other provider handles Personal Data for its own legal or operational purposes, that provider is treated as an independent third party. The Company will not provide Personal Data to an independent third party without the individual's prior consent, except where permitted by the APPI or other applicable law. The Company will provide the information required by law and obtain consent at the relevant stage where required. Personal Data may also be disclosed to courts, customs, tax or other governmental authorities where required or permitted by law, or transferred in connection with a merger, business transfer or other business succession as permitted by law.

6. Handling of Personal Data Outside Japan

The Company uses service providers located outside Japan. The offer-reception and inquiry forms may be provided using Typeform, operated by Typeform S.L. in Spain, and Typeform may engage subprocessors in other countries. Other escrow, payment, cloud, insurance, logistics or technology providers may also handle Personal Data outside Japan.

The Company provides Personal Data to a recipient outside Japan only in accordance with Article 28 of the APPI, including where the recipient is located in a country recognized as having an equivalent personal-information protection system, where the recipient has established a system to continuously implement measures equivalent to those required under the APPI, or where the Company has obtained the individual's informed consent. Where consent is required, the Company will separately provide the information required by law, including the relevant country, its personal-information protection system, and the measures taken by the recipient, before obtaining consent.

Where the Company relies on a recipient's equivalent measures, the Company will periodically confirm the implementation of those measures and take other steps required by the APPI. The Company also understands the personal-information protection systems of countries in which Personal Data is handled and implements necessary and appropriate security measures.

7. Retention and Deletion

The Company retains Personal Information only for the period reasonably necessary for the purposes stated in this Policy, including the following considerations:

  • Initial Offer and non-selected Applicant information: for the selection process and a reasonable period thereafter for inquiries, records, fraud prevention and dispute management.
  • Buyer, Sale Contract, payment, escrow, shipping, customs, tax and accounting information: for contract performance, legally required retention periods, and applicable limitation or dispute periods.
  • Marketing information: until consent is withdrawn or the subscription ends, plus a reasonable period necessary to retain evidence of consent and withdrawal.
  • Identity-verification documents held by an Escrow Provider: in accordance with that provider's applicable retention policy; the Company retains only the limited verification results and identifiers necessary for its purposes.

After the applicable period, the Company securely deletes the information or processes it so that the individual can no longer be identified, unless continued retention is required or permitted by law.

8. Security Measures

The Company takes necessary and appropriate measures to prevent unauthorized access to, leakage, loss, alteration or destruction of Personal Data. The principal measures include:

  • adopting internal rules, identifying responsible personnel, and maintaining reporting and response procedures for incidents;
  • limiting personnel who may handle Personal Data, providing instructions and supervision, and imposing confidentiality obligations;
  • controlling access to offices, devices, accounts and media, and securely disposing of data and media when no longer required;
  • using access controls, authentication, communications security, backups and other technical measures appropriate to the nature and risk of the data;
  • selecting and supervising contractors and reviewing their security measures; and
  • understanding the personal-information protection systems of foreign countries where Personal Data is handled and taking measures based on that external environment.

Further information on the Company's security measures will be provided without undue delay upon a legitimate request, to the extent disclosure would not impair security or other protected interests.
